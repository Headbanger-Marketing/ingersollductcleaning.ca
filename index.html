FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Ingersoll homeowners ask us most.

How often should an Ingersoll home have its ducts cleaned?

For this housing stock, every two to three years is the working rule, with renovations, pets, or a pest episode moving the date closer. The before and after photos from your first cleaning will tell you honestly when the next one is due.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every job we take. Ontario does not issue a trade licence for duct cleaning, so the meaningful credentials are certification, training, and insurance, and all three are ours to show you.

How do I know the cleaning actually happened?

Photos of the inside of your ducts, taken before and after on site and reviewed with you before we pack up. We replace faith with photographs.

Slow dryer lately. Do I need a service call on the machine?

Start with the vent. Old basement paths around Ingersoll collect lint until the machine cannot move air, which looks exactly like a dying dryer. We clear the pipe and measure at the hood, and if the appliance looks responsible we will say so and save you the cleaning you do not need.

What does a whole-home cleaning cover, and how long?

The system is put under negative pressure, registers are removed and washed, each branch is brushed under suction, the blower compartment is cleaned, and photos document everything. Most Ingersoll homes are a three to five hour visit.

The vents smell when the furnace starts. Fixable?

Completely. The odour rides on residue that a full cleaning removes, and a sanitizer applied to the clean surfaces finishes the job. In that order it does not return with the next season.

Why does our house dust over immediately after cleaning?

That pattern points at return-side leaks drawing cellar and cavity air into the system. We can inspect and seal exactly that, which is cheaper and more permanent than repeated cleanings.

Do you work beyond Ingersoll itself?

Yes. Woodstock, Tillsonburg, Beachville, Thamesford, Plattsville, and Norwich are all regular territory for us. Rural addresses, just confirm when booking.

Do you serve areas outside of Ingersoll, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Ingersoll and nearby communities including Woodstock, Tillsonburg, Beachville, Thamesford, and the surrounding area.
